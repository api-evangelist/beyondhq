# BeyondHQ

> BeyondHQ is a distributed workforce planning platform that combines talent, cost, and location data to enable cross-functional decision making for hybrid and distributed teams. It equips HR, Real Estate, and Finance teams with a single integrated experience that centralizes hiring and location-planning across 500+ markets, 70,000+ searchable job skills, and 50,000+ market-data combinations. BeyondHQ is a portfolio company of Bloomberg Beta.
